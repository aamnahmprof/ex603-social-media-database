# Unit 5 Findings

Posts that never received any likes should be investigated to understand why they received no engagement. Leadership could check whether they were removed, flagged, posted at a low-traffic time, or simply never reached an audience. The goal is to understand why there was no interaction and determine whether anything could have been done differently.

Posts that receive likes but consistently perform poorly need a different approach. Leadership should look at what separates them from better-performing posts, such as content, media type, posting time, or average dwell time. They could use those comparisons to make changes to future posts and improve engagement.

The main difference is that posts with no likes need an investigation into why they received no engagement, while posts that receive some likes but perform poorly need improvements to their content or visibility.
